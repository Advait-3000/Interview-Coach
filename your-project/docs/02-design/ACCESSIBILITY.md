# Accessibility Requirements

- All fields have persistent labels; placeholders are not the only labels.
- Full keyboard navigation works through forms, answer choices, dialogs, and tables.
- Focus is visible and follows a logical order.
- Text and controls have adequate contrast.
- Icons are accompanied by accessible text or tooltips where meaning is not obvious.
- Error messages identify the field and how to fix it.
- Do not rely on color alone to convey score, correctness, or weakness.
- Use readable font sizes and support operating-system scaling.
- Test the main flows without a mouse.
