# Product Vision

## Vision
Give students one organized desktop workspace to practise interview questions, take mock assessments, and understand their progress without manually tracking results across many websites.

## Audience
College students preparing for internships, placements, technical interviews, aptitude rounds, and basic HR interviews.

## Value proposition
Practice, results, and progress history are kept together. Students can choose a topic and difficulty, receive immediate objective-question scores, and see which topics deserve more revision.

## Success signals for the MVP
- A new student can register and complete a practice session without assistance.
- A completed assessment is scored consistently and saved.
- A student can revisit previous attempts and see topic-wise performance.
- An administrator can add, edit, activate/deactivate, and remove questions safely.
- The app builds and runs on the documented local setup.

## Non-goals
AI interviewer, speech recognition, resume parsing, online proctoring, company-specific live content, and cloud multi-tenant hosting.
