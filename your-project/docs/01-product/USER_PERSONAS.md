# User Personas

## Persona A — Student preparing for placements
**Goal:** practise Java, OOP, DBMS, DSA, and aptitude questions and understand weak topics.  
**Pain points:** scattered resources, inconsistent tracking, not knowing what to revise next.  
**Needs:** quick practice, clear score, history, understandable topic analysis.

## Persona B — Student doing a mock assessment
**Goal:** simulate a small placement-style test.  
**Pain points:** no consistent scoring or saved result.  
**Needs:** clear instructions, progress through questions, submit confirmation, result summary.

## Persona C — Faculty/project administrator
**Goal:** maintain a reliable question bank for the demo.  
**Pain points:** editing seed data manually is error-prone.  
**Needs:** validated question editor, category/difficulty fields, correct-answer selection, active status.

## Accessibility and environment assumptions
Users may have limited Java experience, use laptop trackpads, or have different screen sizes. Use clear labels, keyboard navigation, readable contrast, and non-color-only status cues.
